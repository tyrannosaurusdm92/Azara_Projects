Aza’ra • The World Beneath the Veil

Aza’ra is a shattered, disc-shaped world suspended in the Void. Four continents, Dakaroth, Arzuhan, Tzaphaer and Belavadós, share the Veil, a mostly transparent magical dome. Floating islands drift above; a fractured cone twists beneath. Its sun is dead or nearly dead, and the Veil illuminates three moons. Nothing survives outside the Veil.
