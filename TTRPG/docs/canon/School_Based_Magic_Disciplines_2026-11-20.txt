🧭 Magic School System  -  Full Ruleset
🔹 1. School Overview
Each magic discipline belongs to one of the ELEVEN schools of magic:
   * Holy, Spirit, Illusion, Nature, Elemental, Arcane, Chaos, Blood, Necromancy, Dream, Infernal
School Determines:
   * Spell behavior and thematic flavor
   * Strengths and weaknesses in combat encounters
   * MMORPG-only skill books, shared training objects, and version-specific advancement paths
   * Trait synergies for spellcasters
   * No School Lock - players may freely mix magic types, creating hybrid casting styles
Magic Skill Progression:
   * Each school functions as an independent skill tree, tracked separately
   * Magic skills level independently from adventuring level, capping at 20
   * School Affinity increases with use, granting passive bonuses such as faster casting speed, critical spell chance, or unique resistances
Corruption Meter & Soul Imprint (Endgame Tier IX+)
   * Certain unsafe or coercive cross-school experiments may build corruption through concrete risks; using multiple schools alone does not automatically create corruption
   * High Corruption can cause instability effects (hallucinations, backlash)
   * Corruption also affects mutation paths, particularly for Chaos/Necromancy casters
   * At high mastery (Tier IX+), players may bind a magic school to their soul
   * Grants permanent traits and exclusive abilities
   * Defines a character’s magical legacy, persisting across reincarnations and lineage systems

CANON UPDATE — TWO GAME VERSIONS AND DOMAIN-FAITHFUL PANTHEON
20 November 2026 | This edition specification supplements the original eleven-school descriptions above. The two versions share the magic vocabulary and pantheon, not identical gameplay, chronology or book dependencies.


1. AUTHORITATIVE HISTORY AND PANTHEON RULES
The Uxrowo Cataclysm is a separate event in 1351 BCE. The Ruqorakai / Iron Dominion rises during 65–213 CE; its hybrid dark-matter/antimatter bomb completely destroys Belavadós, not Tzaphaer, in 276 CE; the Iron Dominion finally collapses in 289 CE. The Classical pantheon era spans 5 BCE–827 CE, but that does NOT mean Belavadós survives past 276 CE. The Traditional era begins in 828 CE; Modern begins in 1798 CE.
The pantheon source is “Aza’ra’s Pantheon (Book),” including its five-era roster and preserved long-form biographies. Its 32 distinct deity identities are represented in the deity atlas below, using the current names and domains. Historic divine titles in the original magic-school draft must not silently replace these identities or be treated as separate gods. In particular, Eirzunet and Eirzunet-Eirna are distinct; Nefarokir and Anubriosh have distinct claims to death; Oskar'enlil and Oskar'enlil-Tyrom are distinct; Raeshkul and Raeshkul-Elysh are distinct.
Domain linkage is not identical to patronage, ethical endorsement or being personally available. A deity may be worshipped, opposed, researched or invoked as a boundary guardian in a school. “Exiled” means exclusion from recognized divine society or public institutions, not death or removal of all power. Ancient, Archaic and Classical deity records must use Exiled rather than Diminished for the affected early-era cases. Traditional and Modern retain their own statuses, including Diminished where the source explicitly records it. “Demigod” is a lineage/ascension category, NOT a fixed limit on influence or power.
No deity may grant powers contrary to their domains merely to fill a spell school. Nefarokir guides rightful death, burial and protection of souls; he does not approve forced undeath or slaughter. Anubriosh's pre-fall Chaos/Death role must not be confused with his later necromantic fall. Eirzunet-Eirna is the source of the FIRST TRUE WILD-MAGIC and surpasses Anubriosh in Chaos and Death despite demigod status. Ruqore's justice is not automatic validation of the Dominion's decrees. Divine magic access must be era-gated, especially for later-born or later-ascended beings. The sources date Nephthysra's birth to 140 CE; Asethyr and other descendants cannot precede their parents. Valsera begins the Ancient and Archaic eras as NOT YET DIVINE and ascends only during Classical; Eirzunet-Eirna is born afterward. Never retcon all of these deities into the start of 65 CE.

2. SHARED ELEVEN-SCHOOL ENGINE
Schools: Holy, Spirit, Illusion, Nature, Elemental, Arcane, Chaos, Blood, Necromancy, Dream and Infernal. This is ELEVEN schools; “Twelve Schools” in legacy headings was a counting error. Each school has independent proficiency, the existing skill cap of 20, distinct practice actions, contextual costs, and compatibility with school mixing. No forced one-school class lock.
The strength/weakness chart in this document governs any +25%/−25% school matchup modifiers if that balance system is enabled. The introductory lists have been reconciled to it; neutral matchups receive no modifier. These percentages remain design parameters to test, especially for competitive play, and are not claims that an objective cosmology literally makes one god superior to another. Effects must resolve by school and spell, not by indiscriminately making all healing or defense deal 25% more damage.
Technique data should include: school ID, spell/ability ID, level gate, learned source, physical or magical target, casting intent, resource cost, effect and duration, counters, environmental conditions, deity/domain context, moral consent constraints, narrative consequence, and version availability. A domain correspondence provides a quest/ritual/lore route; it never unlocks every spell for free.
The existing Corruption Meter and Soul Imprint are retained as separately tuned systems, not a judgment that Chaos, blood-based healing or Necromancy automatically makes a person evil. Reckless coercion, soul violation, unsafe channeling or knowingly broken oaths can cause consequences; ethically performed funeral protection, healing or consensual ritual does not carry the same automatic penalty. Eight-axis Covenant alignment, public reputation, divine standing, school skill and corruption are separate values. Accessible nonverbal casting, keyboard and controller alternatives, captions, readable choice labels and a complete action log are required for both editions.
The original school entries above describe training objects and three tiers of books. Those book titles and level bands belong ONLY to the MMORPG. “Rise of Ruqorakai” uses the same schools and objects but NO books, book UI, reading quests, book loot or book-gated learning.


3. VERSION A — AZA’RA MMORPG (MULTIPLAYER, PVP, BOOKS)
Identity: a persistent online role-playing world of surviving Aza’ra after the 276 CE destruction. Its accessible continents include Tzaphaer, Arzuhan, Dakaroth and other canon-supported surviving lands, with skyships, trains, ferries, submarines, caravans and multiple portals. Belavadós may exist in historical memories, preserved stories, old maps or flashback material; never as an intact explorable contemporary continent. The City of the Mourned Gods is founded after the catastrophe, not in a pre-276 zone. Traditional and Modern divine standing and technology are applied according to the chosen live-world date.
Books have TWO different meanings here. (a) The original eight-book Aza’ra: Threads of Fate saga is supported as story material, historical lore, quests, characters and optional major narrative arcs in the MMORPG. Game experiences may reference and adapt the books without turning every player into Pix or overriding the story's established timeline. (b) Skill Books are magical advancement resources. The named school books already catalogued above cover levels 0–5, 6–10 and 11–15. Levels 16–20 require a high-mastery progression plan with appropriately authored advanced manuals, mentors and trials; names and drop rates for that additional tier remain proposed, not established canon.
PvP IS ALLOWED. Provide both PvE and PvP rules: formal duels, faction conflict objectives, battlegrounds and designated contested regions, with clearly visible PvP flags, understandable opt-in rules, level scaling, anti-griefing protections, safe towns, spawn protection, combat logging safeguards and an accessible no-PvP gameplay route. PvP participation must not be necessary to obtain core story endings, essential mobility tools, basic training or medically relevant accessibility features. Friendly fire, theft and reputation consequences must be explicit. Combat against NPCs is PvE, not PvP.
For PvP, resolve magic schools with server-authoritative resources, range, line-of-sight, collision, status resistances, interrupts, control immunity windows, healing caps and cooldowns. Do not allow a target-locking illusion or blood effect to remove a human player's agency indefinitely. The listed ±25% chart must be tested for uneven matchups; use PvP-specific normalized coefficients if needed without changing a school's lore. Guild politics, crafting, trade, settlement development, rescue services, player housing, public transport and faction reputation remain meaningful alternatives to combat.
Domain-faithful deity play: a healer can serve Sokhivar through volunteer infirmaries, not by farming kills; a ferryman of Nefarokir safeguards burial, not undead enslavement; a Marduthor crafter repairs machinery but can publicly oppose its weaponization; a Ruqore knight may investigate corrupt law; a Vulkiris devotee can support collective liberation but remains accountable for harm. Exiled worship may involve secret groups, dangerous scrutiny or contested shrines. Diminished Modern cults can persist with few devotees without being portrayed as powerless.
MMORPG use cases: cooperative Spirit cleansing in a hospital; guild-led Nature restoration on a scarred surviving continent; an Arcane engineer maintaining a moon-powered skyship; a limited PvP duel between legalist and insurgent players; a noncombatant who resolves a region-wide shortage by trading, diplomacy and harvest magic. Book-canon quests and skill-book drops are enabled only here.
Save/state: account-bound characters; multiplayer guild and party memberships; server-recorded inventory, acquired skill books and learned techniques; character year/era; reputations and eight-axis scores; deity relations and status gates; PvP flag and penalties; trade ledger; permanent quest decisions. Offline progress, when offered, requires authorized reconciliation with the server.


4. VERSION B — RISE OF RUQORAKAI (SINGLE-PLAYER, NO PVP, NO BOOKS)
Identity: a single-player open-world, MMO-scale RPG with exploration, professions, NPC schedules, equipment, factions, branching quests and persistent consequences, experienced entirely through descriptive text and explicit choose-your-own-adventure decisions. Its freedom of travel, character builds, faction allegiances and radiant side quests should capture the open-ended feeling of a Skyrim-style adventure, but the interface is a text-driven story engine rather than a 3D real-time world. “MMO” describes the scale and systems, NOT a multiplayer service. There are no other player characters, no player-vs-player combat, no multiplayer matchmaking and no PvP switches.
The primary campaign is set on living Belavadós during the Iron Dominion's 65–213 CE rise and expansion, before the fixed 276 CE catastrophe. The player may serve Dominion interests, investigate industrial abuses, join independent settlements, practice a profession, support dissidents, become a mediator, or avoid imperial politics while pursuing personal goals. None of these roles presumes a canon name or gender for the player character. NPC conflict, wildlife threats, duels against NPCs, political resistance and environmental danger are PvE/story encounters, never PvP.
ABSOLUTELY NO BOOK SYSTEM: no skill books, spell tomes, collectible reading objects, library/codex reading quests, unlock-by-reading mechanics or adaptation of the novel series into this campaign. Knowledge travels by conversation, witnessed instruction, apprenticeships, practice, experiment, remembered oral teaching, dreams/visions, ritual observation and environmental discovery. The player's journal, quest tracker and accessibility transcript are functional interface records, not in-world books. The school book titles above never appear as items or prerequisites in Rise.
Open-world loop: (1) choose or revisit a known location, with travel time and risks; (2) read a contextual scene of 120–350 words as appropriate; (3) choose 2–6 clearly distinguished actions, always including a safe decline/leave option where practical; (4) check skills, fatigue, tools, relationships, social authority, local time, alignment and deity/domain conditions; (5) resolve consequences with visible fairness, including success with cost; (6) update NPC memory, households, resources, faction suspicion, revealed clues and regional access; (7) surface independent follow-up leads rather than forcing the player back into a single main quest. Keyboard, screen reader, font scaling and complete non-timed choice access are mandatory.
Proposed knowledge progression without books: at school rank 0–5, learn from a living mentor or a first observable exercise; at 6–10, complete applied training, guided field work or repeated independent practice; at 11–15, perform advanced school challenges and combine learned patterns; at 16–20, solve an ethical and technical mastery trial with relevant NPCs, deities or environments. Techniques persist as learned capabilities, not possessions. School rank still caps at 20. Every training object listed above becomes an interactive scene or physical practice apparatus, not a book reward.
The Iron Dominion is an actor, not a mandatory alignment. Joining it may grant access to workshops, transport and power while exposing the player to repression and complicity; resisting it may earn local trust while closing official gates; reform or neutrality may succeed in a district but cannot erase the fixed historical catastrophe. Players can determine who lives, what records survive, which communities escape or reconcile, and how their own life ends, without rewriting the canon destruction of Belavadós in 276 CE. If the calendar continues beyond 213 CE, the story explicitly enters escalating pre-catastrophe years. After 276 CE, Belavadós has no intact playable surface; an epilogue can follow survivors elsewhere. The final collapse in 289 CE is fixed.
Divine presence in Rise is period-correct. Divine contact primarily manifests as dreams, oaths, weather, moral choices, oral traditions, rituals and agents whose behavior fits the deity's recorded domains. The engine can offer a related god's aid, warning, opposition or silence; not every patron personally manifests. Later-born beings remain unavailable before their canonical origin. No deity, no matter how powerful, should simply appear to erase political consequences, grant every school or prevent the 276 CE event.
Choice engine data model: scene_id, year_ce, region_id, location_id, character_build, learned_school_skills, attributes, fatigue, equipment, companions, local NPC memory, faction standings, eight_axis_covenant, deity_relation_state, flags, settlement_state, visible_actions and consequence_log. A choice has prereqs, success/failure branches, immediate costs, delayed changes, quest triggers and exit routes. Seeded deterministic randomization supports replay without making the fiction inconsistent. Store slots and autosaves must be reversible for accidental clicks but preserve deliberate consequence once confirmed.
Example branching scene — Belavadós industrial riverport, 121 CE (illustrative, not a newly established canon city): the workshops have dumped residue beside a struggling neighborhood; a rail engineer asks the player to keep production running while residents report illness.
(A) Consult Hathruna's growers and Sokhivar's healers: investigate living damage, establish a treatment plan, earn local trust, risk costly delays.
(B) Work with Marduthor's artisans and Setrimir's mechanical methods: inspect the process, design a filter, discover whether the harm was negligent or deliberate.
(C) Petition Ruqore's magistrates or challenge a Nebyrr-style binding decree: choose due process, gather witnesses, risk obstruction by officials.
(D) Quietly assist Vulkiris-linked dissidents or Oskar'enlil-Tyrom's covert methods: disrupt the dumping operation, possibly endanger innocent workers and affect faction suspicion.
(E) Seek Bastveig's waterwardens or Horundar's transporters: redirect a threatened ferry route and protect river communities.
(F) Leave or pursue unrelated local work: the neighborhood problem persists and NPCs remember the absence.
This example shows a choice-driven world, not six gods handing out interchangeable spells. Branches may converge at a geographic hub while retaining divergent NPC memory and consequences.
Rise's ending is based on a personalized constellation of choices and relationships: reformer, dissident, loyalist, caretaker, maker, explorer, spiritual mediator or a mixture. The historical timeline is anchored; the player's narrative is not railroaded. No PvP; no books; no mandatory novel protagonists.

The per-deity MMORPG and Rise effects below are implementation proposals grounded in the pantheon, not new canonical divine powers.
Status sequence is Ancient | Archaic | Classical | Traditional | Modern. “Not attested” means the person is not placed in that era's roster; do not invent a birth date. All six demigods are retained as distinct entities; lineage class does not determine strength.

8. DEITY-BASED CLASS AND SUBCLASS SYSTEM — 14 ARCHETYPES, 70 PATHS
DESIGN STATUS — NEW IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION, NOVEMBER 2026
The first 13 archetypes correspond to deities with a recorded Primary or Secondary classification in at least ONE of the five pantheon eras. A FOURTEENTH explicit exception is Anubriosh, the Exiled Thorned Reclaimer: his early-Classical Secondary influence and formidable post-fall necromantic divinity justify a distinct archetype. This does not change the pantheon's late-Classical Exiled or Traditional/Modern Diminished standing. Neither Exiled nor Diminished status means weak or powerless. These are cross-era archetype eligibility rules, NOT claims that all 14 deities are currently Primary/Secondary or were universally worshipped at one date. Deities otherwise only Tertiary, Exiled, Diminished or Demigod remain in their canon roles rather than being silently promoted; they still inform the wider school system. Pantheon: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DY9G6KN637-K7T6hNBCuawaUGYUuh-hjE59JNX6dOHY/edit
The names and actions of the 14 player classes below are new GAME DESIGN PROPOSALS, not claims that those class orders canonically existed. Class fantasy follows each god's actual domains and may be expressed as inspiration, philosophy, martial tradition, cultural training, ritual inheritance or optional patronage. A character does NOT need to worship a deity to train its archetype; a deity does not automatically endorse a player's actions. All 70 combinations have separate progression routes and may be freely combined. Anubriosh's post-fall necromancy is exceptionally powerful even though he is Exiled (late Classical) and Diminished in status (Traditional/Modern).


8.1 THE FIVE UNIVERSAL SUBCLASSES — NO ROLE LOCKS
Steelborn = melee / tank. Weapons, active guard, armor, positioning, intercepting threats; combat by stamina and practical technique. A class's domain changes its style but does NOT make every Steelborn a full spellcaster.
Wildshot = archer / projectile specialist. Bows and era-appropriate ranged equipment, precision, line-of-sight, mobility and tactical target selection. Domain effects are tied to skill, equipment, context or explicitly learned magic, never automatically applied to every arrow.
Spellsword = half-caster. Weapon plus ONE limited school technique or ward in the same action cycle; equal importance to melee and magic. A Spellsword may train other schools but its signature hybrid efficiency requires investing in this path.
Psionist = psionics user. Focus-powered perception, telepathy when permitted, inference, resistance and narrowly bounded kinetic or mental effects. Not synonymous with spell magic: a Psionist can operate with low Ether but must use Focus, concentration and valid target conditions. Private thoughts and truth are not automatically accessible.
Etherist = full / mostly magic user. Ether-intensive school casting, protective rituals, spatial or environmental influence, support or control based on the god's actual domains. Needs preparation, resource management, interruption rules and counterplay.
All five are ways of practicing each deity archetype. A Ruqore Steelborn and a Hathruna Steelborn are both capable of defending a frontline, but one uses lawful intervention and storm discipline while the other uses roots, soil and cultivation. Likewise each god has a distinct Etherist specialization, not eleven schools granted wholesale.
Players can progress Steelborn + Wildshot + Psionist + Etherist simultaneously; Spellsword can coexist with any combination. They can simultaneously train pathways of DIFFERENT deities. The game never forces a character to pick exactly one subclass, one deity, one school, or one damage category. Character custom names, pronouns, lineage, equipment and morality are independent of class labels.


8.2 SKYRIM-INSPIRED PROGRESSION, ADAPTED TO AZA'RA
The attached Skyrim systems research informs the general chain: meaningful activity raises an individual skill; increasing a skill contributes to character leveling; level-ups award a scarce perk choice; prerequisites offer specialization; weapon, defense, crafting and spell skills coexist without forced class exclusion. That is design INSPIRATION only: this is NOT Skyrim's perk list, Dragon Shouts, gods, base 18 skills or school system.
There are three separate, persistent skill structures: (1) CLASS-SUBCLASS PATH rank, one 0–20 rank per deity and role (14 × 5 = 70 independent paths), (2) SHARED MAGIC SCHOOL mastery, 0–20 for the EXISTING ELEVEN Aza'ra schools; (3) UNIVERSAL ACTIVITY skills such as one-handed, two-handed, blocking, precision archery, stealth, healing fieldcraft, smithing, diplomacy, survival and crafting. Increasing one never silently erases another. Skill rank measures practiced proficiency; perk investment chooses named extra capabilities.
PROPOSED starting profile: character level 1, 6 base attributes each 5 with 6 free attribute points, 3 unspent pathway perks, all skills at rank 0 until taught or attempted under safe conditions. Every level after 1 grants 2 freely assignable attribute points and 1 general perk point; every fifth character level grants an additional specialist perk. Perks may be banked. No ability tree claims ownership of the permanent attributes.
Proposed attributes: Might (melee impact and load handling); Endurance (Health, protection and effort); Precision (ranged accuracy, safe target selection); Intellect (Ether capacity and controlled casting); Will (Focus, concentration, resistance); Presence (leadership, cooperation, social leverage). Derived starting pools, tunable: Health=80+8×Endurance+2×Will; Stamina=50+5×Endurance+3×Might; Ether=40+6×Intellect+3×Will; Focus=35+6×Will+2×Precision. These are new Aza'ra tuning proposals, not Skyrim numerical facts. Every role can gain from more than one attribute; no stat choice disables melee, archery, psionics or casting.
Proposed rank thresholds: rank 1 = practice action; rank 5 = first modifier/perk node; rank 10 = specialist action; rank 15 = synergy node enabling cross-path combos; rank 20 = class-role capstone. A character may LEARN the rank-1 action through a trial; upgrades require matching path rank and purchased perks. Advancement at rank 20 pauses until the player elects mastery renewal: optionally reroute refunded perks at an appropriate mentor while preserving skill rank, instead of artificially resetting a rank-20 school to force more leveling.
PROPOSED skill-XP: successful, meaningful, appropriately difficult actions grant 1–8 practice XP to the employed path, 1–5 XP to any genuinely used supporting skill and 1–4 XP to the specific magical school if a spell is cast. Next-rank threshold for path rank r is 10+6r practice XP (r from 0 through 19). Whenever a class-role path or shared school RISES by one rank, grant character XP equal to 4×new rank; character level L advances when the accrued XP reaches 20×(L+3), carrying surplus forward. The formulas are Aza'ra proposals; their purpose is to reward varied, valid gameplay. Merely firing at air, healing already-full targets or repeating a safe loop without consequences yields no XP. Design tested against mixed classes so the player does not become overleveled only by idle crafting.
Universal skills contribute character XP at a calibrated rate but cannot double-count the same real action in several skills at once: choose one primary action track and at most two genuine secondary tracks per encounter. The path matrix remains separately available to every player; specializing deep is cheaper than mastering all 70. Account/global level has no declared mandatory final cutoff; progression after mature mastery can continue via new meaningful roles, while each path's rank remains capped at 20 until a separately approved expansion.
Perk budget: Rank 1 practice is acquired via the class test. Rank 5 purchase costs 1 point, rank 10 purchase costs 1, rank 15 purchase costs 2, rank 20 capstone costs 2 (six points to maximize a role after unlock). Role-specific improvements and shared school perks draw from the SAME finite general pool. Purchasing one branch never spends another branch's banked points. A class can have all five roles active, but finite perk allocation balances generalist flexibility against specialist capstones.
Class identity and divine favor are SEPARATE variables. A political event can lower a deity's public status; the character's trained melee, archery, psionics and other skills remain. The class never raises a deity's canon rank or forces worship. Relationship standing may alter a ritual, audience or special quest but cannot erase hard-earned universal ranks.
Resources and actions: melee strikes cost Stamina, psionic concentration costs Focus, spells cost Ether; hybrid Spellsword actions pay the stated split. Suggested baseline: normal strike 0–6 Stamina, heavy strike 10–15, basic psionic technique 10 Focus, ordinary spell 10–20 Ether, specialist action 20–35 of its resource, capstone 40–55 and/or a narrative setup condition. Costs scale with capacity, cooldown, equipment and accessibility settings and must be playtested, especially against multiple encounters. A character may wield bow plus wards, shield plus mind techniques, or spell plus melee in a SINGLE BUILD; however only ONE major capstone can resolve in a single action window. Offensive cross-school effects do not multiply each other's modifiers indefinitely: resolve at most one positive school advantage and one role synergy at a time.
Player safety and fairness: no permanent unavoidable enthrallment; no forced nonconsensual body or soul change; corpse rites respect Nefarokir's ethical limits; PvP effects are bounded and visibly telegraphed. Equal access to mobility-compatible interaction, screen readers, optional time limits and menu-only controls is a baseline, not an unlockable perk.


8.3 THE CLASS TRIAL, MULTICLASS, TRY-BEFORE-YOU-KEEP AND REMOVAL QUEST
At any hub with a qualified mentor, community practitioner, relevant shrine/cultural tradition or equivalent NPC teacher, select “Explore another archetype.” The interface shows every available class's identity, five role branches, domain philosophy, practical costs and timeline gates. Players are never asked to discard their current build first. Every trial is narrative-first, with noncombat alternatives where relevant; some are guild lessons rather than divine encounters.
Stage 1 — OBSERVE: investigate an exemplar who practices the requested archetype; choose who to ask, how to assess a demonstration and what ethical problem the class would solve. No unlock or fee is required to inspect.
Stage 2 — SAMPLE: play a safe introductory sequence with all five roles available as temporary techniques (Steelborn defense, Wildshot ranged answer, Spellsword hybrid move, Psionist perception, Etherist magic). Choose any two modes, or more if desired, and experience resource consumption and consequences. Sample state is temporary, without permanent perks, and does not overwrite the player's present class.
Stage 3 — DECIDE: pass a domain-appropriate practical or ethical task. Results can be success, partial success with cost, or failure with a follow-up path. The player can keep the new archetype (all five role ranks begin at 0 except the practiced starter role, which opens at rank 1), keep only a selected branch active, or simply leave. Trial completion does not require swearing loyalty to or worshipping a deity. Optional reputation consequences come from actual conduct, not selecting a class name.
Each class has its own signature trial in its profile below. A player's first archetype may start with its test/tutorial completed; a second and later archetype uses the full quest. Multi-classing is unlimited in CLASS COUNT as a design rule; perk scarcity and equipment/runtime cost provide balance rather than hard locks. All learned school masteries, universal skills, inventory, reputation and character level stay intact.
“Try another class” should also be available from the journal of choices when a player discovers an unfamiliar action in ordinary adventures. If a branch is gated by era/birth, the UI explains it without inventing an earlier deity: a player can still study non-divine techniques of the archetype where historical tradition supports them.
“Retire a class” is a separate QUEST, not a punitive one-click deletion. At a mentor or sanctuary select a dormant class and complete an Unbinding of Practice scene: (a) perform one last safe skill demonstration, (b) confirm which class and any role paths should go dormant, (c) review all perk refunds and disabled signature abilities, and (d) choose retire or cancel. Retiring refunds 100% of purchased perks from the selected paths and removes the class from active loadout; it does NOT remove Health/attributes, shared skills, independent school magic, inventory, overall levels or past story consequences. Its learned class-rank progress remains recorded as dormant and reactivation starts with a short return-to-practice quest, not a fresh grind. The player may also retire a SINGLE subclass path while keeping the other four.
Reallocating attributes is not silently bundled with class retirement. A separate optional “Reconsider Training” milestone quest can redistribute all unspent and previously allocated BONUS attribute points (not mandatory starting baseline), with a preview of all derived resource changes and a cancel option. This prevents an experiment with a healer from permanently ruining an archer/psionic build while respecting consequential decisions.
Suggested loadout distinction: “learned” paths are all retained; “active” signature class paths remain available across the whole character, with a soft limit of three equipped passive aura effects to prevent uncontrolled stacking. Active offensive/utility role techniques are NOT limited to three classes. Each action specifies the equipment and resource requirements. If the current outfit cannot wield a bow and shield simultaneously, switching actions consumes the appropriate time and may be unsafe during an encounter.
A player can choose a Ruqore Steelborn + Sigrananna Wildshot + Raeshkul Psionist + Sokhivar Etherist + Hathruna Spellsword build; no arbitrary veto exists. Specialization gives earlier capstone access; generalization gives a broader set of tactical choices.
For MMORPG: the class quests are account/character-bound, have server verification and use the existing SKILL BOOK system only for school training where appropriate; PvP perks use normalized separate balance coefficients. Other players may cooperate in a trial but can never cause an irreversible class retirement for someone else. For Rise of Ruqorakai: the same system is a SOLO branching textual quest with NPC mentors, spoken instruction, hands-on demonstrations, memories and visions; NO BOOKS, no other player characters and NO PVP. Trials happen at historically available pre-276 Belavadós locations, and no later-born deity is physically present before their birth. Test choice outcomes, not reaction-time button prompts.

8.18 14 — THORNED RECLAIMER (ARCHETYPE OF ANUBRIOSH)
Anubriosh is the exception to the thirteen-deity Primary/Secondary roster. He is extraordinarily powerful as the GOD OF NECROMANCY despite divine exile and later diminished PUBLIC STANDING. The word “diminished” classifies his later era rank, not his raw necromantic strength or his ability to heal, decay, reclaim souls and perform difficult resurrection. Canon: before his Classical fall, Anubriosh governs CHAOS AND DEATH, with passage and communication as subordinate domains. After Valsera ascends, Eirzunet-Eirna is born and surpasses him in Chaos and Death, gains Forbidden Knowledge and creates the first TRUE wild-magic. Only then does he fall, relinquish the old portfolios and become the thorn-bearing god of NECROMANCY, soul reclamation and restorative transformation. Nefarokir alone retains rightful, inevitable death. Anubriosh's Modern/Traditional Diminished status and home in the City of the Mourned Gods must not be projected backward into Classical. His class can be practiced without worship or a divine contract.


Domain-linked schools: Necromancy (PRIMARY SPECIALTY, including death-energy healing and reclamation), Spirit (thresholds and souls), Blood (vitality transfer and restoration), Infernal (an OPTIONAL mechanical descriptor for post-fall coercive curse rites, not proof Anubriosh is literally infernal). His BEFORE-FALL techniques are Chaos and Spirit/Death thresholds, not necromancy. Etherist and Spellsword remain the main magical expressions; Steelborn, Wildshot and Psionist apply the same domains to close protection, ranged pressure or psionic perception without becoming obligatory full casters. This archetype creates no twelfth school.


CLASS QUEST — THE EXILE'S HOURGLASS
Observe: find a necromantic hospice, funerary sanctuary or contested memorial community with firsthand testimony. A mentor explains that grieving, healing, reclaiming a soul and enslaving it are different acts. No deity's presence is guaranteed.
Sample: test a protective melee stance (Steelborn), a distant thorn-shot (Wildshot), a weapon plus necrotic ward (Spellsword), the perception of residual soul-echoes without reading private thoughts (Psionist), and a carefully bounded restoration rite (Etherist). Players can sample any two or all five.
Decision: protect someone who is alive, seek consent or a legitimate purpose before soul reclamation, and confront a practitioner who treats an unwilling soul as property. A successful, partially successful or failed attempt branches to different follow-up quests. Accept this archetype, activate chosen paths, or decline without losing existing ranks.
Rise of Ruqorakai temporal gate: if the player is in the early Ruqorakai rise BEFORE Anubriosh's Classical fall, the trial is instead THE HOURGLASS BEFORE THORNS, covering his existing Chaos/Death threshold tradition. Black Tearbrand and all post-fall necromancy/resurrection techniques are LOCKED until the narrative's confirmed fall event flag is reached; the document does not invent a precise date of that fall. The game must not conjure a post-fall Anubriosh or his later city before history allows it. A character can still learn unrelated nondivine magical skills from ordinary mentors.
Multiclass: Thorned Reclaimer paths can be combined with any of the existing thirteen deity archetypes and all five role styles. There is no class-role exclusivity, and the standard trial, voluntary retirement, full perk refund, dormant rank retention and reactivation rules apply.


UNIQUE CLASS FEATURES — EXACT SIGNATURE RULES
BLACK TEARBRAND — classwide, post-fall passive with explicit activation toggle. Dark streaks beneath the character's eyes deepen visibly as necromancy is used. While active, increase ALL applicable healing done by +10% and all necrotic damage dealt by +10%; do not multiply the +10% separately for each of the five subclass paths. Drawback: reduced resistance to divine disruption. Proposed balance value is −15% divine-disruption resistance while active (a tunable figure, not author-established canon). Healing and damage bonuses remain +10% exactly as specified. Dismissal clears the aura but does not instantly erase an in-progress divine backlash. Interface must describe effect, visual intensity, and defense tradeoff.
INVOCATION — THORNVEIL ASCENSION — post-fall, high-mastery class invocation. Channel Anubriosh for EXACTLY 15 seconds, massively strengthening healing, necrotic decay and eligible resurrection/reclamation rituals. The exact major-boost percentage is intentionally a balance parameter; optional initial prototype is +50% healing and necrotic damage with improved decay persistence, subject to tests. For EXACTLY 30 seconds AFTER channeling ends, the caster suffers −20% healing effectiveness AND −20% damage dealt; the debuff cannot be canceled merely by swapping a class loadout. If interrupted, resolve a defined proportional cost and do not grant full benefit. Activation requires a real mastered Thorned Reclaimer path and a quest unlock; it never overrides canon deaths, soul consent, resurrection preconditions or the timeline's fixed destruction. A summoned or recalled ally is not necessarily the original deceased soul.
TEARS OF THE EXILED — post-fall restorative ritual, with ritual tears automatically collected FOR ALLIES rather than requiring manual loot pickup. When a valid healing, reclamation or protective grief rite produces a Tear, eligible allied characters automatically receive or collect it. Consuming/triggering a Tear restores HP and provides TEMPORARY NECROTIC RESISTANCE. Suggested test values for a single Tear are 8% of the ally's maximum HP plus +15% necrotic resistance for 15 seconds, with up to three unspent Tears stored per ally and a reasonable creation cooldown; these secondary numbers are proposed implementation defaults, not user-provided fixed canon. The ally's active condition and consent-to-buff preference are respected; no dead target is auto-revived by ordinary Tears. Tears are not an inventory book or a material to be farmed from forced suffering.
SOUL SHATTER (MELEE TOUCH) — post-fall close-range technique, NOT a ranged arrow or a remote psionic mind command. On a valid hostile enemy within genuine melee touch range, the caster rips free the enemy's soul, dealing MASSIVE NECROTIC DAMAGE and applying FRACTURED WILL. Against eligible hostile NPCs, Fractured Will FORCES that opponent to fight as the caster's THRALL for EXACTLY 60 SECONDS, after which control ends; the NPC can then recover, surrender or return to hostility according to AI and quest state. Elite or quest-protected enemies may require weakening, a successful resistance contest or special consequences, but do not silently discard the 60-second thrall duration after successful full application. Existing commands, target selection and allegiance shift are shown to the player. The effect is hostile coercion and may severely affect divine reputation, NPC trust and faction standing. It can never be used to enslave ordinary bystanders or unconsenting companions without explicit quest consequences.
MMORPG PvP counterplay: a human PLAYER is NEVER transformed into an automated 60-second thrall or made to attack teammates against their inputs. The PvP version preserves the melee touch, heavy damage and Fractured Will name, but applies bounded, resistible short disruption with a visible defense/cleanse opportunity. The full, exactly 60-second forced-thrall effect is reserved for eligible NPC/PvE targets. Rise of Ruqorakai has no PvP at all, so hostile NPCs receive the full intended 60-second thrall mechanic. No passive perk can turn Soul Shatter into ranged mind control.


FIVE THORNED RECLAIMER SUBCLASSES — EXACTLY ONE OF EACH
Steelborn — Thorned Bulwark: front-line melee, defensive shrouds and soul-touch counterplay. Rank 1 “Graveguard Stance”: block a hit and turn the absorbed energy into a small self-heal, consuming Stamina. Rank 10 “Reclaimer's Hold”: physically pin a hostile threat while warding an ally from necrotic splash; costs Stamina plus a limited Ether reserve. Rank 20 “Soul Shatter — Thorned Judgment”: unlocks the exact melee-touch Soul Shatter described above after the related quest; the eligible NPC enemy becomes a thrall for 60 seconds under Fractured Will. This role specializes in closing safely to touch range, not permanent mind control.
Wildshot — Thornspike Harrier: ranged reconnaissance and careful necrotic pressure, never a ranged Soul Shatter. Rank 1 “Gravebloom Arrow”: fire a thorn-tipped arrow that marks a hostile NPC for tracking, costing ammunition and Stamina. Rank 10 “Withering Quiver”: add a bounded post-fall necrotic decay effect to accurate shots, consuming an Ether charge if magical. Rank 20 “Funeral Volley”: loose a field of thorn projectiles against an advancing mob while leaving a clear retreat route for allies. Ranged afflictions cannot trigger forced-thrall Soul Shatter; the caster must still close to melee touch.
Spellsword — Thornveil Reaver: close combat plus bounded necromantic recovery and vitality exchange. Rank 1 “Mourning Edge”: blend a weapon strike with a small restorative ward and pay both Stamina and Ether. Rank 10 “Tearbrand Parry”: parry to form one Tear of the Exiled for an endangered ally if the Tear ritual is learned; this does not stack the +10% Black Tearbrand bonus twice. Rank 20 “Hourglass Severance”: deliver a charged close-range necrotic cut, then transfer a portion of eligible damage dealt into healing for wounded allies, subject to a soul-integrity check.
Psionist — Soul-Remnant Listener: insight into residual memories and the psychology of soul-fracture, without granting omniscience or permanent telepathic domination. Rank 1 “Last Echo”: use Focus to detect a nearby, unclaimed soul-imprint or mournful resonance, preserving privacy limits. Rank 10 “Fracture Reading”: identify whether a hostile target could resist the close-range Soul Shatter, revealing tactical risk rather than controlling the target at distance. Rank 20 “Witness Between Veils”: guide several willing mourners through a safe memory-sharing ceremony and reveal a concealed soul-binding hazard. Focus pays for perception, not a substitution for necromantic Ether cost.
Etherist — Exiled Ascendant: full Necromancy casting, restorative ritual tears, soul reclamation and difficult resurrection. Rank 1 “Thornward Renewal”: spend Ether to restore HP using death-energy reclamation without requiring a fresh killing. Rank 10 “Tears of the Exiled”: unlock ally auto-collection and temporary necrotic resistance for appropriate healing rituals; an Etherist may also lend the ritual to another learned Thorned Reclaimer path through shared proficiency. Rank 20 “Thornveil Ascension”: unlock the exact 15-second massive boost and the mandatory −20% healing/damage weakness for 30 seconds afterward; the ability remains classwide when its requirements are met and can coexist with other trained classes.
Classwide loadout access: Black Tearbrand is a toggleable post-fall archetype feature after the introductory rite, not five stackable passives. Tears require completion of the rank-10 Etherist ritual or its separately approved advanced cross-path teaching quest. Soul Shatter requires the Steelborn rank-20 mastery OR an equivalently demanding post-fall classwide advanced quest plus the necromancy prerequisites, so a trained Etherist or Spellsword who multiclasses is not arbitrarily denied melee-touch use. Thornveil Ascension requires Etherist rank 20 OR a comparably rigorous post-fall classwide invocation quest with at least one mastered rank-20 Anubriosh role and the needed Necromancy skill. All unlock routes use the shared finite perk pool; they never duplicate rank points or turn Steelborn into a full caster by default.

1. Anubriosh remains late-Classical EXILED, Traditional/MODERN DIMINISHED in standing and extremely capable in necromancy; exile does not mean imprisoned or disempowered. Preserve before-fall Chaos and Death and after-fall Necromancy as distinct temporal states.

4. Thornveil Ascension lasts 15 seconds; aftermath is −20% to healing AND damage for 30 seconds; changing classes does not wipe the penalty.

6. Soul Shatter requires melee touch; valid hostile NPC becomes a Fractured Will thrall for exactly 60 seconds. In the PvP-enabled MMORPG, a human player instead receives bounded, counterable PvP control, not 60 seconds of forced automated play.
7. The MMORPG retains optional PvP and existing skill books. Rise retains no PvP and NO books and cannot teach post-fall Anubriosh necromancy in a pre-fall year. Do not retroactively change the canonical 276 CE destruction of Belavadós or the 289 CE final collapse of Ruqorakai.
