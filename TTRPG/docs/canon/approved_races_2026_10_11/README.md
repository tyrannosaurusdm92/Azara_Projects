# Aza'ra approved race and bloodline canon, 11 October 2026

The accompanying plain-text file is the full race reference. It covers 22 documented species, peoples, and mixed-heritage entries. Species, cultures, and ancestry combinations are distinguished rather than automatically counted as 22 separate species.

**Approved physical canon**
- The amphibious people have always been Dril'thar, without a separate ancestral species.
- Azh’raim is the ancient name of the same nine-bloodline people known in the modern era as Azrahai. The exact name-change year remains undefined.
- Jaspey Mosscap, Gloomtail, and Rotcap appearances use their approved surface characteristics.
- Unmixed Aza'raans are very pale to lightly tanned, commonly copper through dark auburn-haired and silver, blue, or green-eyed; oceanic cheekbones, slight ridges, and long backward-pointing ears are defining traits.
- Pix's pointed ears come from his father. His mother, Jacob, and Clarahmizah do not have pointed ears. The maternal protector line has extensive Gandirosha and Zirramunis heritage, accounting for the less recognizable Aza'raan appearance.
- Kaluseban, Deorbasun, Zirramunis, Iamhazira, and Gandirosha may contribute inherited variation to mixed Aza'raan families.

The language grammar and glyph inventories remain unchanged. The original user-supplied archives and raw race-history documents remain intact as research materials, not active canon. No previous integrated build is treated as a source.
