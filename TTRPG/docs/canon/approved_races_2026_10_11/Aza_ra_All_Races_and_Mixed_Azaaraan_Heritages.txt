AZA'RA: APPROVED PEOPLES, PHYSICAL APPEARANCE AND BLOODLINES

Character creation reference for the Book Series, TTRPG and MMORPG

Canon revision: 11 October 2026

==============================================================================

STATUS AND TERMINOLOGY

All physical descriptions and physical subtype details in this edition are approved by the author. The twelve strict-registry species remain distinct from other source-documented peoples and mixed-ancestry communities. A mixed community is not automatically another biological species. The source’s character name samples remain proposed, not established personal names.

Azh’raim is the ancient name of the draconic people who are now called Azrahai. Both names describe the same species and its nine established bloodlines. The specific year of simplification is not established.

Dril'thar has always been the sole name for the amphibious people. Its six environmental bloodlines belong exclusively to Dril'thar history.

Unmixed Aza’raans have very pale to lightly tanned skin, usually copper or auburn hair and commonly silver, blue, or green eyes. Family heritage can introduce further variation.

Real-world human population references are visual color-range analogies only, not Aza'ran ethnic identities.





I. STRICT-REGISTRY SPECIES

==============================================================================



01. Dril'thar

--------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Small to modestly sized amphibious people, usually about one to four feet tall, with sinewy limbs,
damp protective skin, webbed hands and feet, and often a powerful prehensile tail. Sensory fins or
frills can frame the head. Large reflective eyes, color-shifting patterns, and luminous markings
permit both speech and visual expression. They can move upright or on four limbs; moisture is
essential to their health.

Dril'thar has always been the sole name for these amphibious peoples and their lineages. No alternate ancestral species name is part of canon.

Recorded forms and bloodlines:

* Grassland: Light sky blue, pale tan or sun-washed gold surfaces adapted to open wet meadows. Legs remain strong
for leaping and traversing shallow water.

* Lush Forest: Moss green, bark brown and lichen gray coloring; fine rootlike patterns help bodies blend into
shaded wet woodland.

* Marsh: Peat brown, slate gray and murky green mottling, often with a denser protective skin coating for
stagnant wetlands.

* Rainforest: Vivid emerald, sapphire or violet skins carrying bright bioluminescent rainlike markings and
pronounced sensory frills.

* Ocean: Ocean lineages may be slightly larger, with elongated swimming profiles, reflective seafoam or deep
blue skin, and strong tails.

* Reef: Brilliant reds, turquoise, luminous pink and textured coral-like patterning; fins and webbing
support reef-water movement.

Name-design influences: Ancient or Old Egyptian, Navajo (Diné Bizaad), Taa (!Xóõ / !Xoon)

Name pattern: Optional CLICK + CV.CV or CV.CV.CV; do not require a click in every name

Sample proposed character names: ǃKhemaru, ǀSenaki, ǁNamaru, ǂTsenahe, ʘMekanu



02. Kaluseban

--------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Tall, slender, graceful magic-dependent humanoids with features that vary markedly among bloodlines.
Skin may range from pale ivory through earth green and brown to deep sapphire. Eyes glow faintly in
the color of their affinity, while fine hair can shimmer or float in dense ambient magic. Appearance
should communicate personal lineage, not a single uniform skin color.

Recorded forms and bloodlines:

* Psionic: psionic light in the eyes or temple markings can be an individual appearance cue.

* Swamp: Wetland greens and browns with reed-like markings are characteristic possible looks. The historic
text describes woodland and marsh-linked ancestors, but not every detail of this current label.

* Arcane: Luminous pale or violet skin and shifting magical markings are among the approved options for an
arcane lineage.

* Dark: Low-light, shadow-touched coloring and dim luminescence can occur in this bloodline.

* Stone: Source describes granite, sandstone and muted moonstone coloration, crystalline highlights, and
markings reminiscent of stone inscriptions.

* Oceanic: Pearlescent aquamarine or tide-blue color and water-adapted accents are approved visual options.

Name-design influences: Korean, French

Name pattern: (C)V.CV.CV; usually 3-5 syllables

Sample proposed character names: Yermifana, Meryefina, Salmyeoro, Gyerfima, Constefaria



03. Azrahai

------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Ancient and modern naming: Azh’raim gradually simplified to Azrahai across generations. The precise transition date has not been established. This is a historical name change within one species, not the merger of two species.

Physical appearance: Original Aza'ran draconic people whose living anatomy includes scales, a powerful tail, clawed
hands, facial ridges and, where appropriate to lineage, wings. Bodies range from heavy mineral-
armored to long and streamlined. Ancient records call these same people Azh’raim; their modern name
is Azrahai.

Recorded forms and bloodlines:

* Noitzideq: Tall with layered green or blue-green scales, gold and dark speckles, feather-edged aerodynamic
wings, and a muscular tail.

* Saelftum: Massive, volcanic-adapted build with charcoal or black scales, amber and red accents, broad wings,
horns and molten-looking fissures.

* Jazwed: Coarse red-green iridescent scales, crystal clusters, dense muscular frame and shorter gliding wings
adapted to heat.

* Khoalaros: Long aquatic body with dark reflective scales, bioluminescent veins, webbed extremities, dorsal fins
and swimming-capable wings and tail.

* Gauksneth: Pale sand-colored crystalline scales, slim limbs and long sail-like wings suited to arid terrain.

* Tuorszom: Gray, brown and black layered scales with mineral streaks, protective plates, and broad climbing
hands and feet.

* Taudinax: Dark iridescent scales, semi-translucent patches, silent wings, elongated fingers and a flexible
tail.

* Gouxelq: Deep blue metallic scales, crystal protrusions on shoulders and knuckles, heavy frame and broad
flight wings.

* Vautaces: Smooth ruby-red and cream scales with radiant crystal edging, long fingers, elegant wings and fine
facial ridges.

Name-design influences: Georgian, Japanese

Name pattern: CV.CV.CV or CVC.CV.CV

Sample proposed character names: Zarikome, Korotavi, Merazuki, Tavikoro



04. Panolima

-------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: People whose bodies visibly reflect one of five elemental affinities, rather than a separate generic
Elementaris species. Some are solid as stone, some carry ember patterns, others appear fluid, airy
or illuminated by magical energy. Their silhouettes and voices may change with the environment; none
of those forms should be mistaken for a separate current species.

Recorded forms and bloodlines:

* Earth: Bark or stone textured skin, mosslike hair, weighty build, earthy fissures and a deep resonant
voice.

* Fire: Glowing ember veins, heatlike shimmer and hair that can suggest flame; specific color and physiology
vary.

* Water: Fluid, reflective skin and flowing hair or limbs that resemble currents; water effects need not
imply constant liquid anatomy.

* Air: Light, wind-swept features and shifting translucency, with clothing or hair that seems moved by
unseen breezes.

* Aether: Luminous magical auras, irregular light patterns and other non-elemental arcane features.

Name-design influences: Icelandic, Swahili

Name pattern: CV.CV.CV or CVC.CV

Sample proposed character names: Svalinuru, Tharakima, Nurathara, Kimasvali



05. Jaspey

-----------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Jaspey are recognized as one people with three established forms, Mosscap, Gloomtail, and Rotcap.
Each form has identifiable surface markings or anatomy. Their shared underlying body plan is not
specified in the surviving current reference, so no extra height, limb count, or facial anatomy is
invented here.

Recorded forms and bloodlines:

* Mosscap: A moss-textured head or crown, with soft green-brown highlights.

* Gloomtail: A shadow-adapted tail or low-light markings.

* Rotcap: Dark fungal or decay-associated patterning.

Name-design influences: Basque, Thai

Name pattern: CV.CV.CV or V.CV.CV

Sample proposed character names: Zurimai, Keriluna, Mailuri, Lunkerai



06. Deorbasun

--------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Tall to towering magical shapeshifters, sometimes feline or beastlike, whose physiques vary from
muscular to lithe. Fur, scales or earth-toned skin, reflective eyes, wings, tails or other animal
features vary according to the individual's soul bond. No single default animal form applies to
every member.

Recorded forms and bloodlines:

* Open Soul-Animal: This is an open choice, not a preset wolf, cat or bird subtype. Record the person’s initial
appearance and update markings or silhouette only after the true soul bond is established.

Name-design influences: Quechua, Turkish

Name pattern: CV.CV.CV or CVC.CV

Sample proposed character names: Kirasuni, Arutalka, Sunikira, Talkaeru



07. Gandirosha

---------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Wiry or lithe agile figures, roughly three to six feet tall in the detailed source, with softly
luminous skin and shifting freckle-like or sigil markings. Hair can range from honey and copper to
chestnut, jade and gold. Eyes may glow subtly when magic is active; layered clothes and charms suit
travel and performance.

Distinct named biological forms: none supplied in the current race registry.

Name-design influences: Irish, Yoruba

Name pattern: CV.CV.CV, sometimes initial consonant groups

Sample proposed character names: Goraeni, Sefamuni, Enigora, Munesefa

weightless hair and shifting auroral features. Their eyes may appear to perceive several layers of
reality. Intangibility and visual fluidity vary with magic and should not be assigned as a

Distinct named biological forms: none supplied in the current race registry.

09. Geisamahi

--------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Small, upright, ferret- or stoatlike people with compact wiry bodies, elongated muzzles, expressive
eyes, twitching whiskers and opposable thumbs. Fur may be brown, russet, orange, snow white or

Distinct named biological forms: none supplied in the current race registry.

10. Tamhiogal

--------------

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Amphibious aquatic people with dramatic betta-fish coloration: brilliant blues, violets, teals, reds
and shifting iridescence. On land they have scaled humanoid legs and arms, bioluminescent jellyfish-
like hair, reflective eyes and scales at the face and neck. In water, gills, webbing, dorsal fins
and a long caudal fin become prominent. They need hydration and tidal energy.

Distinct named biological forms: none supplied in the current race registry.

Name-design influences: Arabic, Polish

Name pattern: CV.CCV.CV or CV.CV.CCV; 2-4 syllables

Sample proposed character names: Nurfaleya, Mirszanira, Zawadira, Falnurina, Mazrelya



11. Aburata

Classification: strict JSON species

Physical appearance: Infernal magical beings with a humanoid and a quadrupedal equine-inspired form. Humanoid skin can

Distinct named biological forms: none supplied in the current race registry.

II. OTHER SOURCE-DOCUMENTED PEOPLES

==============================================================================



01. Aza’raan

-------------

Classification: established Aza’raan people in race document, outside strict JSON

Physical appearance: Aza'raans are generally tall, slender and graceful, with long limbs and a very pale to slightly tanned complexion. The usual unmixed skin-color range resembles fair to lightly tanned complexions seen among Italian, Slavic and Balkan human populations, without implying any shared Earth ethnic identity. Copper, deep copper, auburn and dark auburn are the most common hair colors. Brown appears sometimes, while black and blonde are rare. Silver, blue and green eyes are common, often with a distinctly oceanic shape, occasionally dual-toned or subtly reflective. High oceanic-style cheekbones, a slightly angular brow with shallow paired ridges, and long ears swept backward are defining traits. Ear tips range from moderately pointed to dramatically sharp and elongated, with individual variation. Some Aza'raans bear faint freckles, subtle psionic luminescence, or wave-like ritual markings. Kaluseban, Deorbasun, Zirramunis, Iamhazira and Gandirosha ancestry can introduce additional color, stature, ear, hair, eye, animal-associated, or magical characteristics; such variation does not redefine the typical unmixed appearance.

Distinct named biological forms: none supplied in the current race registry.

Approved Aza’raan heritage appearance rules:

* Aza’raan with Kaluseban ancestry: Kalus’aan is the established name for this mixed-ancestry community. Their Aza’raan cranial ridges,
slender limbs, silver, blue, or green eyes and copper-to-auburn hair may mingle with Kaluseban color
shifts, luminous marks, mineral textures, water-adapted accents, or forest and shadow features. The
six documented Kalus’aan expressions are Arcane, Starborne, Wildroot, Tideborn, Stonebound, and
Umbral; they are not six separate species. Appearance differs with the family and the particular
Kaluseban bloodline.

* Aza’raan with Deorbasun ancestry: An Aza’raan descendant of a Deorbasun family may keep pale-to-lightly-tanned Aza’raan skin and
auburn or copper hair while expressing more pronounced reflective eyes, feline or other soul-animal
hints, a heavier mane, unusual ear contours, tail, fur patches, claw-like nails, or rare wing
features. The Deorbasun soul-animal bond is individually expressed and cannot be assigned a standard
animal or fixed bloodline by appearance alone. No separate canonical community name has been
established.

* Aza’raan with Zirramunis ancestry: These descendants may be shorter or more densely built than a typical Aza’raan, with strong
forearms, compact musculature, prominent bone structure, mineral-glinting eyes, stone-colored accent
markings, and thick hair or beard growth. Copper and dark auburn hair and the silver, blue, or green
Aza’raan eye spectrum can persist, with jeweled or metallic notes from Zirramunis heritage. No
distinct mixed-species name or special fixed subtype is confirmed.

* Aza’raan with Iamhazira ancestry: The historically documented Aza’zira community blends Aza’raan facial proportions, psionic markings,
and reflective eyes with Iamhazira height, powerful musculature, endurance traits, braids, strong
jaw structure, and sometimes tusk features. Complexion may extend beyond the unmixed pale-to-
lightly-tanned baseline where that parental lineage is visible. Individuals may retain copper or
auburn hair, or express their Iamhazira family’s tones and scar or ornament customs.

* Aza’raan with Gandirosha ancestry: These descendants can retain the typical pale or lightly tanned Aza’raan complexion, copper or
auburn hair and silver, blue, or green eyes, while expressing greater agility, a more compact or
lithe build, freckles that resemble luminous sigils, more mobile facial expression, or colorful hair
highlights associated with Gandirosha ancestry. These are variable family traits, not a separate
named species or mandatory personality features.

IV. CANON CROSS-REFERENCE AND LIMITATIONS

==============================================================================

The three distinct official Jaspey appearances are Mosscap (moss crown, green-brown highlights), Gloomtail (shadow-adapted tail or low-light markings), and Rotcap (dark fungal or decay-associated patterns). No extra Jaspey limb count or facial anatomy is established.

The strict registry confirms twelve species and thirty designated variants, bloodlines, or forms. Zirramunis, Garuraqets, Iamhazira, and Aza’raans are documented by the current MMORPG race material but are not part of the twelve-species strict JSON list. Existing mixed communities remain separate cultural identities, not necessarily new species.

The name Azh’raim in ancient passages and Azrahai in modern passages must not be changed to a single spelling everywhere. It is a genuine historical simplification.

The Aza'raan language grammar and glyph system were not modified for this appearance update.



V. PIX AND THE PROTECTOR BLOODLINE

==============================================================================

Pix family appearance canon: Pix is a transgender man who can pass as human despite his visibly pointed, backward-swept ears. The childhood name 'Pix' came from those conspicuous ears, which his parents explained as an old inherited abnormality. Pix and his father have the tell-tale pointed ears. Pix's mother, Jacob and Clarahmizah have ordinary-looking human ears without points. Pix's mother eventually explains that much of the protector bloodline shared by her and Clarahmizah has Gandirosha and Zirramunis ancestry, and many descendants lost the conspicuous Aza'raan ear shape. Pix inherited the unmistakable ears through his father. The absence of pointed ears does not erase anyone's Aza'raan lineage or protector heritage.
