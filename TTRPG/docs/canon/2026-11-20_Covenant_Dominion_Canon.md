# Belavadós: Eight-Axis Covenant and Iron Dominion canon (2026-11-20)

## Source precedence
1. Direct author correction 2026-11-20: **Belavadós, not Tzaphaer, was destroyed in 276 CE** by the Iron Dominion's anti-matter/dark-matter weapon. Any archival wording to the contrary is superseded.
2. Attached `Azara_Eight_Axis_81_Canonical_Alignment_Types(1).json` supplies the **81 exact names**, 8 axes, 0–3000, 5 bands and the "TRUE NEUTRAL" profile.
3. Attached `Azara_Eight_Axis_Covenant_Desktop_Mobile_Game_Specification(1).txt` sets the current five-band rule and prohibits involuntary identity-based alignment assignment.

## Fixed historical anchors
- 65–120 CE: Ruqorakai/ Iron Dominion arcano-industrial rise in Dakaroth; widespread steam, skyships and rail.
- 102–145 CE: Iron Dominion conquest of Zephonarai, with named settlements in the authoritative timeline.
- 182 CE: Theraeon's exile, amid Classical divine upheaval.
- **276 CE: Ruqorakai detonation destroys Belavadós**; Tzaphaer is **not** the detonation target.
- **289 CE:** final political collapse of the Iron Dominion.
- 313–360 CE: successor powers take shape in the wider aftermath.
- **828 CE:** Traditional Era begins. It is not the date of the Belavadós detonation.

## Divine constraint
There is **no divine diminishment before the Traditional Era**. Historical pre-Traditional losses of public standing are **Exile**, without automatic death, loss of divine powers, captivity or residence in the City of the Mourned Gods. Traditional and Modern may use the Diminished standing. The pantheon runtime roster, including all 32 deity identities, has been corrected without removing names.

## Alignment model
Eight independently scored integer axes: Altruism, Lawfulness, Cooperation, Honor, Mercy, Transformation, Autonomy, Restraint. Start 1500; bands 0–749, 750–1499, **1500**, 1501–2249, 2250–3000. First four map to the user's 81 named profiles; last four remain independent expression axes. Pragmatic is the inherited *catalog low-Honor slot* label, not a moral synonym for dishonor.

Population species and ancestry do **not** determine innate moral values. Deities, NPCs, governments, settlements and institutions have independent editable records. Public reputation is separate. A quest or NPC action must carry an explicit scene, evidence and consequences before alignment changes. The legacy 4-axis authoring import can migrate missing axes to neutral without inventing new canonical names.

## Clickable locations
The underlying map geometry stays intact. Clicking a building opens full location information with **actual campaign-clock scheduled NPC occupants**, associated residents/workers/visitors, catalogue goods/services and their prices. Empty businesses have no invented stock; historic Belavadós sites are reconstructions, not claims of active inhabitants after the continent's destruction.

## Editorial separation
The `dominion-history.js` display marks local trade-contracts, spies, agent routes, guild disputes and similar region-specific prompts as **optional GM scenarios**, not already confirmed dated events. The known cross-continental interference and expansion are historical facts, but invented local episodes are not silently promoted to canon.

`TTRPG/ttrpg.html` remains the shell; `TTRPG/assets/code/index.html` remains the only nested HTML. New code and data live under existing `js/`, `css/`, `json/` and `docs/` directories. Maps and NPC data were not rewritten.
