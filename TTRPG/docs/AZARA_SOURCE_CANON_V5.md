# Aza’ra Approved Peoples, Pantheon & Gender Identities

Source of race canon: `Approved Race Canon 2026-10-11` user-provided Drive folder, especially its race ZIP and 16-species/6-heritage registry. Azh’raim is the historical name of Azrahai, not an extra species. Mixed heritages are not distinct species. Original NPC names, buildings, coordinates, addresses, work/school locations and six-day schedules are unchanged.

Pantheon source: uploaded *Aza’ra Pantheon - Book Reference.docx*. There are 32 distinct deities, described under five era-specific indexes (27 ancient, 27 archaic, 32 classical, 32 traditional, 32 modern). Historical rank and worship do not prove that a demigod existed in an earlier era. The six demigods are indexed for completeness; their actual chronology is preserved in the source text. No race is automatically attributed a particular creator deity.

14 permitted gender identities: Agender, Apagender, Bigender, Cis-Female, Cis-Male, Demi-Female, Demi-Male, Gender-Flexible, Genderfluid, Genderless, Nonbinary, Poly-Gender, Trans-Female, Trans-Male. The disallowed label was removed from code and text assets. Gender identity and pronoun selection remain separate fields, and each individual has a specific pronoun paradigm and preferred honorific; these values are synthetic worldbuilding where the source lacked them.

Neutral family language defaults to parent, child, sibling, nibling, pibling, grandparent, and partner. Canon sources for honorific and pronoun vocabulary: https://universalenglish.org/gender-neutral-honorifics/, https://www.genderspecialist.com/blog/gender-neutral-family-language, https://pronounslist.com/.

Map alignment guard: DO NOT edit `assets/maps/elarvess.png`, `assets/maps/elarvess.geojson`, or `js/elarvess_buildings.js`. All original building/coordinate data is still included verbatim.
