# Belavadós Visitable Location Catalogue

Built for dark steampunk D&D 5e settlement templates. Use each list as a generator pool for map pins, districts, NPC homes, NPC workplaces, visiting schedules, services, goods, and reroll tables.

## Generator Rules

- Capital Cities may include the public Portal Network Building; other settlements may have portal customs offices, inactive portal relics, or relay bureaucracy, but not public portal hubs.
- Cities and Capital Cities may include skyship docks, roofports, and skyship yards. Towns and villages may include skyship signal towers or emergency landing meadows only when explicitly added by the DM.
- Underwater settlements replace orchards, greenhouses, and land farms with kelp farms, algae gardens, coral nurseries, pearl farms, silt farms, vent gardens, and aquaculture pens.
- Deep-sea and underwater settlements include a pressure-sealed arrival airlock: guests swim into a water chamber, a pressure door locks, water filters out, and a dry guest concourse opens.
- Underwater stables board aquatic mounts such as seahorses, hippocampi, turtles, crabs, eels, rays, or trained reef-beasts rather than horses.
- Ocean surface settlements replace rail/caravan movement with chain ferries, floating trams, cargo skiffs, steamships, ferries, submarines, and skyship mast-docks where settlement size allows.
- Treetop settlements replace normal horse stables with glider roosts, bird rookeries, rope elevators, canopy lifts, drake aeries, and bridge repair stations.
- Goods and services should live inside the relevant locations, not in one long global list.
