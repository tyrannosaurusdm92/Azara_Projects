Belavadós Time Conversion Guide
Earth-first table play, Belavadós-second lore time
1. The New Rule
Use Earth dates and Earth time first for players, schedules, sessions, travel, and deadlines.
Use Belavadós dates second for lore, temples, festivals, legal records, journals, and immersion.
A Belavadós year is 330.15 Belavadós solar days long. For table play, those 330.15 days are distributed evenly across the Earth year.
2. Clean Math
3. Conversion Formula
daysInEarthYear = 365, or 366 in a leap year.
earthProgress = (Earth day-of-year - 1 + fractionOfEarthDay) / daysInEarthYear.
belSolarDayProgress = earthProgress x 330.15. Use this for sky, moon, season, and astronomy math.
belCivilDateProgress = earthProgress x 330. Use this for the named calendar date.
monthIndex = floor(belCivilDateProgress / 30), from 0 to 10.
dayOfMonth = floor(belCivilDateProgress % 30) + 1.
Belavadós clock time may use the fractional part of belSolarDayProgress x 24.

5. Weekdays
For player-facing schedules, keep the live Earth weekday mapping. This keeps sessions, transit boards, deadlines, and reminders easy to read.
6. Night Sky Integration
Use 330.15 Belavadós days as the astronomical year because the Night Sky file identifies Belavadós at 1.00 AU with a 330.15-day orbital period.
Use 330 named civil dates for the calendar because the civil calendar is still 11 months of 30 days.
Use solar progress, not month/date progress, for moon phases, planetary positions, constellations, tides, and sky omens.
The larger moon Chandratecu-Seleneš has a 19.20 Belavadós-day orbit; the smaller moonlet Mictlindra-Nannara has a 2.02 Belavadós-day orbit. For Earth sessions, calculate their phase from belSolarDayProgress so the sky stays smooth all year.
Because the solar correction is distributed, the Star-River, wandering worlds, and paired moons never need a special frozen/end-buffer period.

8. Formatting Standards
General date: Earth Month Day, Year / Belavadós Month Day, Weekday.
Exact time: Earth time with UTC or local label / Belavadós clock time.
Settlement time: include the settlement UTC if it differs from the province primary UTC.
Province summary: show one or two UTC labels only, never a long chain of every border band.
Sky event: calculate from belSolarDayProgress and display Earth-first for players.
