# Aza’raan Letter Evolution — Interactive Chart

Extract the ZIP and open **index.html**. Keep **images/** beside it. The single-page chart uses the same layout and interaction pattern as the English alphabet evolution program: one six-stage lineage diagram, a **Trace a letter** selector, **Show all**, click-to-inspect glyphs, a copy control, and a correspondence table.

The chart stages are Proto-Aza’raan, Ancient, Archaic, Classical, Traditional, and Modern. Proto-Aza’raan shows the 52 ancestral hieroglyph roots. The independent-letter totals are Ancient 52, Archaic 49, Classical 44, Traditional 32, and Modern 21. Dotted arrows show the supplied descent and merger schedule. **All hieroglyphs and designed glyphs on the evolution chart render white**, with the colored lineage connectors preserved. The underlying glyph image files remain unchanged.

Each ancestral hieroglyph keeps its fixed slot from 1 to 52. The five **images/<era>/** folders retain the individual era glyphs, plus their 12 accent SVGs and transparent accent contact sheets. Glyph artwork was not redrawn or altered for this interface pass. The source register, slot map, merger schedule, and origin images remain packaged beside the app.

The copyable table uses modern Unicode readings. The custom historical era glyphs are SVG artwork without assigned Unicode codepoints. Special signs remain outside the 21 modern letters; the click sign is **¡ (U+00A1)**.

The merger schedule contains provisional stage placements where the source register labels them as working proposals.
