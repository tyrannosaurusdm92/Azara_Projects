## Preservation of both SVG sets

The complete build uses white-ink original lineage SVG drawings for its dark-background interactive chart. The canonical package also contains 15 contrasting black-ink revised drawings. Those alternatives were preserved in `assets/svg/refined-contrast/`, not substituted on the dark chart where they could become invisible.
