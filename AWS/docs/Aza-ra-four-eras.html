Aza’ra

One requested composition, shown in Archaic, Classical, Traditional, and Modern artwork.

Read left to right: /æ/ + subscript z, /ʌ/, then r + glottal stop + subscript /ʌ/. Each cluster touches the next at its safe ink boundary.

Era rules: /æ/ and /ʌ/ have their own independent historical artwork; Modern uses A with Open Vowel and A with Central Vowel. The historical /ʌ/ subscript retains its own artwork, while Modern uses subscript a + Central Vowel. The next-syllable emphasis > exists from Classical onward, so Archaic omits it.
